# 📊 What We Learned from Rocket Mortgage

## Conversion Elements We Adopted

### ✅ 1. Prominent Phone CTA
**Rocket:** `(888) 452-8179` in header, always visible  
**ABL:** `(619) 927-ROCK` in header, hero, apply section, footer

**Why it works:** Mortgage is high-touch. People want to talk to a human. Making the phone number impossible to miss removes friction.

---

### ✅ 2. Multiple "Apply Now" / "Get Started" CTAs
**Rocket:** Every section has a CTA (hero, rate cards, why section, footer)  
**ABL:** "Get Your Rate in 3 Minutes", "Get My Rate" on every rate card, "Apply Now" in hero + apply section

**Why it works:** Different people convert at different points in the page. Give them the CTA when they're ready.

---

### ✅ 3. Rate Cards with Clear Pricing
**Rocket:**
- 30-year fixed: 6.75% rate, 7.046% APR, $2,271/mo
- 30-year FHA: 5.99% rate, 6.818% APR, $2,242/mo
- Each card has: Rate, APR, Monthly Payment, Points, Learn More link

**ABL:**
- Bank Statement: 7.25% starting rate
- DSCR Investment: 7.49% starting rate
- Asset Depletion: 7.75% starting rate
- Each card has: Rate display, feature bullets, CTA button

**Why it works:** Transparency builds trust. People came to see rates — show them rates front and center.

---

### ✅ 4. Trust Signals (Social Proof)
**Rocket:** TrustPilot reviews carousel, 5-star ratings, real client quotes  
**ABL:** Three 5-star testimonials with real first names + locations

**Why it works:** "Other people like me got approved" removes fear.

---

### ✅ 5. Educational Content
**Rocket:** Huge "Learn" section with guides, calculators, FAQs  
**ABL:** "Our Superpowers" section explains each loan type simply

**Why it works:** Mortgage is confusing. Education builds authority and trust.

---

### ✅ 6. Value Props (Benefits Over Features)
**Rocket:**
- "Personalized rates. Lightning fast."
- "No credit impact"
- "No hidden fees"

**ABL:**
- "48 lenders = best rate guaranteed"
- "3 min rate quote"
- "$0 hidden fees"
- "No credit impact • No obligation • No BS"

**Why it works:** Benefits answer "What's in it for me?" faster than feature lists.

---

### ✅ 7. Speed Messaging
**Rocket:** "3 mins or less" to estimate rate  
**ABL:** "Get your rate in 3 minutes"

**Why it works:** Reduces perceived friction. "Fast" = "easy."

---

### ✅ 8. Clean, Organized Rate Table
**Rocket:** Grid layout, easy to compare 6 loan types side-by-side  
**ABL:** 3-column grid (desktop), single-column (mobile)

**Why it works:** Makes comparison shopping easy. People want to see all options at once.

---

### ✅ 9. "Most Popular" Badge
**Rocket:** Highlights 30-year fixed as most common  
**ABL:** Highlights DSCR Investment Loan as most popular Non-QM product

**Why it works:** Reduces decision paralysis. "If most people pick this, I probably should too."

---

### ✅ 10. Mobile-First Responsive Design
**Rocket:** Works flawlessly on mobile (where most traffic comes from)  
**ABL:** Fully responsive, touch-friendly CTAs

**Why it works:** 60%+ of mortgage research happens on mobile.

### Rocket's Flow:
```
Homepage → See rates → Click "Estimate my rate" → 
Multi-step form → Connect with loan officer → Application
```

### ABL's Flow:
```
Homepage → See rates → Click "Get My Rate" OR call →
[Pending: Jotform or phone] → Loan officer contact → Application
```

**Note:** We left application flow flexible (phone-first OR form-first) based on your preference. Rocket leans heavy on their online application. You can do the same OR prioritize calls (which is more BirdRock's style).

## Key Takeaways from Rocket's Success

### 1. Remove Friction
- "3 minutes" messaging
- "No credit impact"
- "No hidden fees"

Every objection is addressed upfront.

### 2. Show, Don't Tell
- Rate cards with actual numbers
- Testimonials with real outcomes
- Trust badges (TrustPilot, BBB)

### 3. Multiple Conversion Paths
- Phone for high-touch
- Online application for self-serve
- Chat for quick questions

Meet people where they are.

### 4. Content = Authority
Rocket has hundreds of "Learn" articles. They own SEO for "mortgage rates", "FHA loan", etc.

**Opportunity for ABL:** Own SEO for "non qm mortgage", "bank statement loan", "DSCR loan" with blog content.

## What We Did BETTER Than Rocket

### 1. Memorable Branding
Rocket is professional but generic. ABL's superhero theme is:
- Instantly memorable
- Shareable (people will screenshot the mascot)
- Ownable (no other mortgage site looks like this)

### 2. Niche Focus
Rocket serves everyone → harder to dominate.  
ABL serves Non-QM only → easier to own "Non-QM superhero" positioning.

### 3. Human Touch
Rocket is a tech company that happens to do mortgages.  
ABL is a broker with personality — feels more like working with a person, not a machine.

## Next-Level Moves (Inspired by Rocket)

### 1. Build a Blog
Rocket dominates SEO because they publish constantly. ABL should do the same for Non-QM keywords:
- "What is a bank statement loan?"
- "DSCR loan requirements"
- "How to qualify for a mortgage when self-employed"

Target: 1 article/week, 50 articles in Year 1 = SEO dominance.

### 2. Add Calculators
Rocket has 10+ calculators (mortgage calculator, affordability calculator, etc.).

ABL should have:
- **DSCR calculator** — "Will this rental property cash-flow?"
- **Bank statement calculator** — "How much can I qualify for with 12 months of statements?"
- **Asset depletion calculator** — "Turn your assets into qualifying income"

### 3. Create a YouTube Channel
Rocket has a massive YouTube presence with explainer videos.

ABL opportunity: **Schoolhouse Rock-style animated explainers** for Non-QM loans. Fun + educational = viral potential.

### 4. Rate Alerts (Like Rocket)
Rocket lets you subscribe to rate drop notifications.

ABL could do: **"Non-QM Rate Watch"** email list — weekly updates on DSCR, bank statement, asset depletion rates.
